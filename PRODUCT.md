# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

KIVA serves people buying specialty coffee online: home brewers, espresso drinkers, and curious coffee buyers who want to compare origin, process, sensory profile, price, and package character quickly.

## Product Purpose

The site presents KIVA as a direct, distinctive specialty coffee roastery and lets visitors browse coffees, understand their profiles, and add products to a lightweight frontend cart.

## Brand Personality

Bold, direct, tactile. The interface should feel confident and physical, with strong contrast, clear product focus, and a little rawness. It should not feel like a generic polished SaaS template.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic beige ecommerce, weak contrast, decorative UI that does not help shopping, over-rounded card systems, AI-looking landing-page tropes, hidden product information, and layouts that make the coffee packages feel secondary.

## Design Principles

1. Let the coffee package carry the visual identity.
2. Keep commerce actions direct and easy to understand.
3. Use strong typography and spacing before decorative effects.
4. Make filters and product comparison feel fast, not heavy.
5. Preserve KIVA's bold, graphic character across desktop and mobile.
6. Preserve SEO value: semantic headings, descriptive product/category copy, crawlable links, stable metadata, accessible alt text, and clear internal paths must survive visual iteration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, touch-friendly mobile controls, visible focus states, and reduced-motion fallbacks for animations. Avoid relying on color alone for meaning.
